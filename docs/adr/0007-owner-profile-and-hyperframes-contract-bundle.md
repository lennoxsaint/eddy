# ADR 0007: Owner profile and HyperFrames contract bundle

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-07-26

## Context

Eddy's generic profile and single `frame.md` did not encode Lennox's repeated corrections about
viewer-leverage windows, evidence routing, semantic layouts, progressive Short captions,
license-safe music and SFX, camera grade, or full-watch repair loops. Model bake-offs also copied
editor instructions without a portable content-addressed design contract.

## Decision

Eddy adds `eddy-quality-profile-v2` and the `lennox-professional-youtube-v1` preset. Explicit
`profile_id` wins over owner-channel configuration; generic creators retain `creator_good_v1`.
The owner installer selects the Lennox preset.

Every run creates `design.md`, landscape `frame.md`, portrait `shorts/frame.md`, local snapshots of
the verified HyperFrames v0.7.3 doctrine, and `contracts/contract-bundle.json`. Normative YAML owns
tokens and geometry. Prose explains intent. Every hero frame is composed and checked statically
before animation.

`eddy-host-packet-v3.1` and `edit-plan-v3.4` bind the profile, design contracts, doctrine, audio
plan, grade plan, caption policy, correction evals, 100-point rubric, and SHA-256 hashes. Legacy
plans through v3.3 remain readable, but cannot claim the Lennox-profile completion state.

The 0-3 second and 0-30 second Viewer-Leverage Windows are hard-gated and repaired first. Three
complete watch/critique/repair passes are the minimum, not a retry ceiling. Red candidates remain
quarantined; repair changes strategy until all evidenced checks score 100 or an exact external or
technical blocker is recorded. A green candidate remains under Owner Taste Lock, and audience
performance remains `NOT_RUN`.

Systemic project design repairs increment the affected contract revision, record the reason and
hash diff, invalidate dependent renders, and rerun adherence checks. Promotion to the owner profile
is proposed at owner approval and never automatic.

## Consequences

- “Eddy, edit this” is creator-aware without launching a bake-off.
- Long and Short compositions share brand truth but have independent frame geometry.
- The complete editing doctrine is portable and hash-verifiable.
- False 100/100 claims fail when any point lacks file, frame, timestamp, hash, playback, or
  measurement evidence.
- Explicit bake-offs can freeze one identical bundle for all contestants without revealing their
  identities to Eddy.
