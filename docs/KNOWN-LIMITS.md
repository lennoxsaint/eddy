# Known limits

- The no-review claim is locked until five diverse real-footage dogfoods are owner-approved green.
- Descript API success alone does not prove Studio Sound survived export. Eddy blocks unless the
  project changed and the returned audio passes provenance, signal-change, and duration checks.
  Echo and voice-texture scores are diagnostic because they are not calibrated Studio Sound oracles.
- Audio-only inputs are unsupported for final video rendering.
- Multi-speaker interview editing has no diarization contract and should block or require a tailored
  host plan.
- RTL/CJK burned karaoke is not yet production-proven.
- Owner installs follow `main`; public users follow stable tags and may lag development intentionally.
- Eddy v3.0 creates videos and receipts only. Packaging and publishing are out of scope.
